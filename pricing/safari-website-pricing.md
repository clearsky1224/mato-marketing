Once the consultation is complete and the final quotation is approved, a **50% deposit** of the total project cost is required to begin website development.

### Step 3: Additional Work & Milestone Payments
Any additional features, revisions, or requests beyond the agreed scope during development will be billed separately as a milestone payment before implementation.

### Step 4: Final Payment & Project Handover
Upon project completion, the remaining balance must be settled before the official handover. Access credentials, website files, and final documentation will be provided after payment clearance.
